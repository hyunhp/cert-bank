Privacy

Short version: cert-bank has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no cookies of its own. Your study progress stays in your browser.

What stays on your device

The question bank, mock exams and study plans save your answers, bookmarks, mock exam history and study progress in your browser's local storage, so they are there the next time you visit. This data never leaves your device unless you export a bookmark backup yourself. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it.

Who else receives a request

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web server it receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads; see the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces on the question bank, study and support pages, so your browser requests them from Google's servers.
  • Images on guide pages are served from hyunhp.github.io (also GitHub Pages).

Payments

Support is optional. The support page links to Ko-fi and KakaoPay; a payment happens entirely on their sites, under their privacy policies. cert-bank never sees card details.
