CA signing keys can be generated and stored in Cloud HSM (FIPS 140-2 Level 3 validated) using Google-owned and Google-managed keys, or you can use an existing Cloud KMS key. W: Subordinates under the external root: trust stays anchored in the offline root, the cloud subordinates do the daily issuance, and their signing keys stay in Cloud HSM.
X: B=Key in a bucket: it would expose the root key outside hardware and destroy the benefit of offline custody.
C=Import issued history: previously issued certificates cannot be imported, and importing an external CA with issued certificates is discouraged.
D=Replace the root: a new root does not chain to the custody-controlled root, so every client must change its trust anchor and the existing custody rules no longer protect issuance.
T: Chain CA Service subordinate CAs to your offline root and keep their keys in Cloud HSM.
