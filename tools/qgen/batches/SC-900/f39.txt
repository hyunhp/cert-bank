S: The IT team cannot patch thousands of vulnerabilities at once. It wants them ranked using threat intelligence, breach likelihood and business context, with remediation tasks sent to Intune. Which service fits?
A: Microsoft Defender for Office 365 Threat Explorer, which ranks email campaigns by how many users in the tenant they reached
B: Microsoft Defender for Cloud Apps, which ranks discovered SaaS apps on risk factors and marks them sanctioned or unsanctioned
C: Microsoft Defender Vulnerability Management, which orders device weaknesses by exposure and hands the fixes to device admins
D: Microsoft Purview Compliance Manager, which ranks improvement actions by their effect on the organization's compliance score
K: C
G: ## Risk-based vulnerability management
A large estate always has more vulnerabilities than a team can fix at once. **Risk-based** prioritization asks which ones matter most: those being exploited in the wild, those on devices with critical data, and those already involved in an active breach.
## Microsoft Defender Vulnerability Management
Defender Vulnerability Management delivers:
- **continuous asset discovery and monitoring** with consolidated inventories of software, certificates, hardware, firmware and browser extensions
- **risk-based intelligent prioritization** using Microsoft threat intelligence, breach likelihood predictions, business context and device assessments
- **remediation and tracking**: create a remediation task in **Microsoft Intune** from a security recommendation, block vulnerable applications, see alternate mitigations and track progress in real time
It covers Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS and network devices.
W: Defender Vulnerability Management: it ranks vulnerabilities by threat intelligence, breach likelihood and business context, and sends remediation requests from security recommendations to Intune for the device administrators.
X: A=Threat Explorer: it investigates email threats; it has no view of unpatched software on devices.
B=Defender for Cloud Apps: its risk scores apply to cloud apps found in traffic logs, not to software vulnerabilities on devices.
D=Compliance Manager: it ranks compliance improvement actions toward regulations; it does not prioritize CVEs on devices.
T: Defender Vulnerability Management finds and prioritizes vulnerabilities by real risk and hands remediation to IT through Intune.
