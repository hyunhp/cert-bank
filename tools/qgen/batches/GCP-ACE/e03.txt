G: ## Quotas protect capacity
Cloud Quotas limits how much of a resource a project can use. Most quotas apply per project, and many are **regional** or **zonal**: CPUs in `us-central1` are counted separately from CPUs in `europe-west1`. When a request would exceed the quota, it fails with a quota error.
## Requesting more
1. Open **IAM & Admin > Quotas & System Limits** (or the gcloud `quotas` commands).
2. Filter to the quota and the region, select it, and choose **Edit quota**.
3. Enter the new value and a justification, then submit.
Requests are mostly evaluated by automated systems, sometimes by people; you get email about the decision. Large increases can take time, so request early.
## Roles
- View quotas: Quota Viewer (`roles/servicemanagement.quotaViewer`).
- Request adjustments: Quota Administrator (`roles/servicemanagement.quotaAdmin`).
A paid Customer Care service is **not** required to request a quota adjustment.
W: Quota adjustment request: the error is a regional allocation quota, which is raised by submitting an adjustment for that quota and region; Quota Administrator gives the permission to submit it.
X: A=Paid support first: the docs state that no paid Customer Care service is needed to request a quota adjustment.
B=Budget amount: budgets track spend against an amount. An alerts-only budget only sends alerts, and a spend cap budget can pause one eligible service (such as Cloud Run), but no budget sets or raises quota values.
C=Remove location policy: the location constraint decides where resources may be created, not how much quota a region has, and the error isn't a policy violation.
T: When a quota blocks you, request an adjustment for that quota and location in Quotas & System Limits (Quota Administrator role); budgets never change quotas, and no paid support is needed.
