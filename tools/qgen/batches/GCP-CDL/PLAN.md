# GCP-CDL batch plan (topics owned per batch)

Exam: Google Cloud Cloud Digital Leader, new version live 2026-08-12, exam guide read 2026-10-30. 90 minutes, 50-60 multiple
choice and multiple select questions, pass mark not published. Target 226 (D1 40, D2 41, D3 41, D4 41, D5 41, D6 22).
Rules: `tools/qgen/GCP.md` (GCP-CDL and GCP-GAIL sections: guide's product names, nothing preview or deprecated, no prices /
limits / plan names / model versions, business-level stems, unreachable hosts listed there). Fundamentals style: a business
goal, then which approach, product or benefit fits; never commands or console steps. About 15-20% "(Select TWO)".
Overlap: GCP-ACE (hands-on, published) and GCP-GAIL (gen AI, published) share product facts. Before writing, read their
published questions in `data/GCP-ACE.json` / `data/GCP-GAIL.json` and use new business scenarios; do not repeat them.
Shared hot spots belong to ONE batch only; others may use them as distractors but not as the tested concept.
