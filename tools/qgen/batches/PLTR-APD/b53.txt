G: ## Refresh token rotation in Foundry

Example: worker 1 takes the lock, refreshes, saves token R2 and releases; worker 2 then reads R2 instead of reusing R1.

T: Foundry rotates refresh tokens and invalidates the grant on late reuse; serialize refreshes and always store the newest token.
