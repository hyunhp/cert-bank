@ GCP-GAIL D2 T2.2 applied definition
S: A freelance designer wants a ready-made AI assistant she can open and chat with to brainstorm taglines, draft emails and get explanations, without any company IT project, servers or code. Which Google offering is aimed at this individual use?
A: The Gemini app, a prebuilt conversational assistant that individuals use directly for writing, brainstorming and learning
B: Model Garden, a catalog where developers discover and deploy models and then build their own applications around them
C: AI Hypercomputer, an integrated infrastructure system for organizations that train and serve large models at scale
D: Agent Registry, a central control plane through which IT indexes and governs the agents built by many teams
K: A
G: ## Prebuilt AI "applications"
The exam's fifth layer of the gen AI landscape is the application layer: finished products people use without building anything. Google's prebuilt offerings for AI-powered work sit here. The **Gemini app** is the one aimed at individuals: an assistant you chat with to write, brainstorm, summarize, learn and create.
## How to tell it from the other layers
- **Applications** (the Gemini app, Gemini Enterprise, Gemini for Google Workspace): used as they are, by people doing their jobs.
- **Platforms and models** (Model Garden, Agent Platform): used by developers who build solutions.
- **Infrastructure** (AI Hypercomputer, TPUs, GPUs): used by teams that train and serve models.
A designer who wants to "open it and chat" needs an application, not a platform or hardware.
W: Gemini app: it is a ready-made conversational assistant for individuals, so she can start immediately with no build, hosting or code.
X: B=Model Garden: it serves developers who select and deploy models, which is a build task, not a finished assistant for a designer.
C=AI Hypercomputer: it is infrastructure for large-scale training and serving, which a freelancer writing taglines does not need.
D=Agent Registry: it is an IT governance tool for agents that already exist, not a chat assistant for personal use.
T: The Gemini app is the prebuilt personal assistant; platforms and infrastructure are for builders.
V: Gemini app

@ GCP-GAIL D2 T2.2 applied use-case
S: A recruiter keeps pasting the same long set of instructions into the Gemini app each time she wants a job description written in the company's style: friendly tone, short paragraphs, fixed section headings. Which feature of the Gemini app removes this repetition?
A: Retraining the underlying foundation model from scratch on past job descriptions so that it learns the company style
B: Gems, custom versions of Gemini that keep her instructions so she can reuse them for this recurring task
C: A longer conversation, because the assistant carries instructions from one chat session into every new chat
D: Switching to AI Hypercomputer, which stores prompt text on dedicated accelerators for faster retrieval
K: B
G: ## Gems in plain terms
A **Gem** is a customized version of Gemini that a user sets up once with instructions for a specific job, such as a writing coach, a meeting-notes helper or a job-description writer. The user then opens that Gem whenever the task comes up instead of re-typing the setup.
## Why it matters to a business
- **Consistency**: the same tone and structure each time.
- **Time saved**: no copying of long prompts between chats.
- **No technical skill**: the person writes instructions in plain language; nothing is trained or coded.
## What Gems are not
A Gem does not retrain the model; it packages instructions around the same Gemini model. That is closer to prompt engineering than to fine-tuning.
W: Gems: they save the recruiter's instructions as a reusable, customized Gemini, so every job description follows the style without re-pasting.
X: A=Retraining a model: far too heavy for a formatting preference and not what Gems do; the instructions are what change, not the model.
C=Longer chats: each new conversation starts without the previous instructions, so relying on chat history keeps the repetition.
D=AI Hypercomputer: it is infrastructure and has nothing to do with saving a user's instructions.
T: Gem = a reusable custom Gemini defined by saved instructions, with no training or code.
V: Gemini app

A start-up's developers want to embed Gemini models in a customer-facing product they are building. Which mapping of group to Google offering is correct?
A: Writer: Gemini Enterprise; HR: Gemini app; developers: Gemini for Google Workspace
B: Writer: Gemini for Google Workspace; HR: Gemini app; developers: Gemini Enterprise
C: Writer: Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform; HR: Gemini app; developers: Gemini Enterprise
D: Writer: Gemini app; HR: Gemini Enterprise; developers: Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform
K: D
G: ## Who each offering is for
- **Gemini app**: individuals who want a ready-made assistant (Gems let them save their own instructions).
- **Gemini for Google Workspace**: people who work in Gmail, Docs, Slides and Sheets and want help inside those apps.
- **Gemini Enterprise**: employees of an organization, where IT connects company data and manages security and agents.
- **Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform**: developers who build their own solutions and applications from models and tools.
## Method
Ask three questions: who is the user (an individual, an employee, a developer), where does the work happen (a chat window, a productivity app, a company-wide set of systems, a custom product), and who manages it (the user, IT or a development team).
W: Gemini app, Gemini Enterprise and Agent Platform: personal drafting, permission-aware company-wide answers, and building a customer-facing product match these three in order.
X: A=Enterprise for the writer: this puts a company-wide product on an individual's drafting need and a personal assistant on an HR system search.
B=Workspace for the writer and Enterprise for developers: the writer did not ask for in-app help in a productivity suite, and developers embedding models need a build platform.
C=Agent Platform for the writer: a developer platform is excessive for a writer's drafting needs, and the personal app cannot serve connected company systems.
T: Individual = Gemini app; employees on company data = Gemini Enterprise; developers building products = Agent Platform.
V: Gemini app, Gemini Enterprise, Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform
