G: ## Choosing hybrid connectivity
Google Cloud offers several ways to link an on-premises network to a VPC network. The choice is driven by throughput, latency stability, availability target and whether traffic may use the public internet.
- **HA VPN** builds IPsec tunnels over the public internet. It is quick to set up and encrypted, but throughput per tunnel is limited and latency varies with internet conditions.
- **Dedicated Interconnect** is a physical connection between your network and Google's at a colocation facility. Traffic stays off the internet and capacity is dedicated, which suits sustained bulk transfers.
- **Partner Interconnect** reaches Google through a service provider when you cannot be in a Google colocation facility.
## Availability is a topology decision
Interconnect availability depends on how many connections you order and where they sit. The documented 99.99% (production-critical) level needs at least four Dedicated Interconnect connections and can be built in two ways:
- **Two or more regions**: two connections in one metro and two in another metro, using both edge availability domains (zone 1 and zone 2) in each metro, with VLAN attachments in two regions.
- **One region**: four connections in a single metro, two in each of a pair of facility and edge availability domain combinations (for example two in `iad-zone1-1` and two in `iad-zone2-5467`), ordered as a Dedicated Interconnect connection group with the `PRODUCTION_CRITICAL` topology, with all VLAN attachments in the metro's low-latency region.
Google coordinates maintenance between the two edge availability domains inside a metro, so only one is affected at a time; it does not coordinate across metros. Both designs reach 99.99%, but only the two-metro design also keeps traffic flowing if a whole metro is lost.
W: Dedicated Interconnect with redundancy across two metros: it keeps the daily bulk ingest off the public internet, gives dedicated capacity for sustained traffic, and follows a documented 99.99% topology that also survives the loss of a whole metro, which matches the stated reliability priority. A four-connection single-metro connection group would also reach 99.99%, but it is not among the choices, and the single-metro choice offered has only one connection.
X: B=Direct Peering: it exchanges public traffic with Google's edge for Google public services and is not a private connection to your VPC network. It carries no Interconnect SLA and is not the recommended design for production hybrid traffic.
C=HA VPN over the internet: it is encrypted, but the traffic still crosses the public internet and per-tunnel throughput is limited, which conflicts with the requirement and with predictable sustained bulk transfers.
D=Single connection plus Classic VPN: one connection in one metro is a single point of failure and does not follow any Interconnect availability topology (the single-metro 99.99% design needs four connections in a connection group). A VPN backup over the internet cannot carry the same bulk volume, so a failure would degrade ingestion badly.
T: Sustained, private, reliable hybrid traffic means Dedicated (or Partner) Interconnect with a redundant topology: 99.99% needs four connections, either in one metro as a connection group or across two metros, and only two metros survive a metro outage, never a single link.
