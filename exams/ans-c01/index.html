D1 · Network Design 30%

Network design scenarios combine DNS, Transit Gateway, Direct Connect, edge services and load balancing across many VPCs, accounts and Regions. You choose the topology or the sequence of steps that meets an isolation, scale or address-overlap requirement. Several options often look workable; the right one enforces the rule at the correct layer and uses the fewest moving parts.

What to watch for

  • For any multi-VPC question, check connection count, transitivity, service versus network level and overlapping CIDRs before reading the options closely.
  • Remember what peering carries and what it does not: transit gateway peering moves traffic, but routes for the remote side must be added explicitly.
  • When a requirement says two networks must never talk, look for separation in route tables rather than relying only on security groups or NACLs.
