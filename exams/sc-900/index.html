D1 · Describe the concepts of security, compliance, and identity 12.5%

Concept questions in this domain use everyday security situations: signing in with a social account, protecting data while it is processed, storing password hashes, segmenting a network, or buying insurance against a risk. You name the concept, layer or response. Several options are true security ideas, so the decision rests on which one the scenario actually describes.

What to watch for

  • Encryption questions depend on the data state: stored, moving across a network, or in memory while it is processed.
  • For defense in depth, decide which layer a control belongs to. Internal segmentation and edge defenses sit on different layers.
  • Learn the risk responses as four separate actions: accepting, reducing with controls, transferring to someone else, and avoiding.

D2 · Describe the capabilities of Microsoft Entra 27.5%

Microsoft Entra questions describe identity requirements in practical terms: a smart card solution, on-premises account states enforced at sign-in, admin password resets, or policy exclusions that need regular checks. You choose the sign-in method, Conditional Access element or governance feature. The correct choice matches the stated constraint, such as where passwords must stay.

What to watch for

  • For hybrid sign-in, list what must happen on-premises: a third-party authentication method and live account state checks point to different methods.
  • Keep Conditional Access inputs and outputs separate: signals describe the request, decisions say what happens next.
  • Accounts that hold Microsoft Entra administrator roles follow their own, stricter SSPR rules, whatever the policy for ordinary users says.

D3 · Describe the capabilities of Microsoft security solutions 37.5%

Security solution items ask what a Microsoft product or feature is for: storing secrets, joining Defender products into one view, telling SIEM from SOAR, explaining secure score behavior, or sending a suspicious email for analysis. Most answers rest on the product's main purpose. Wrong options describe a neighbouring product or a capability from the other half of a pair.

What to watch for

  • Treat SIEM and SOAR as two halves: one collects and correlates, the other automates the response. Microsoft Sentinel provides both.
  • Secure score changes only with recommendations that count toward it. Check the status of a recommendation before expecting a score change.
  • Application secrets belong in Azure Key Vault, read by a managed identity, not in configuration files or code.

D4 · Describe the capabilities of Microsoft compliance solutions 22.5%

Compliance questions test Microsoft's privacy commitments and the Microsoft Purview and Compliance Manager tools. A scenario asks where sensitive data is, how audit records can be kept long term, how assessments cover several clouds, or how Microsoft treats customer data. Accurate answers follow the documented commitment or licensing need; distractors overstate or understate what a tool does.

What to watch for

  • Content explorer and Activity explorer answer different questions: what sensitive data exists and where, versus what people did with it.
  • Long audit retention depends on the audit tier, a retention policy and the right add-on license. Check that all parts are present.
  • Microsoft's privacy commitments say customer data is used only to provide the services customers chose, never for advertising.
