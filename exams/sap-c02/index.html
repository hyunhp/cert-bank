D2 · Design for New Solutions 29%

Designing a new solution means getting each requirement right from the start. Scenarios set a recovery objective, a deployment workflow, a launch with known demand, or a network rule for many VPCs, and ask which pattern or service meets it at the stated cost. The usual trap is an option that works but is either over-built for the budget or too slow for the target.

What to watch for

  • Rank disaster recovery patterns by recovery time, data loss and running cost, then pick the cheapest one that still meets the stated objectives. Each step up buys speed with standing capacity.
  • Plan for limits before a known event. If a launch will need far more capacity than the account normally uses, Service Quotas increases are part of the design, not an incident response.
  • For controls that must apply across many VPCs and accounts, such as an allow-list of domain names, look for a DNS-level filter combined with a service that deploys it organization-wide.
