About cert-bank

cert-bank is a free study site for cloud, data and AI certification exams: original practice questions with explanations, timed mock exams, and day-by-day study plans. There are no ads and no sign-up, and nothing is behind a paywall.

What is here

  • Exam pages: one page per exam with its format, domains and weights, what each domain asks, and a study order.
  • Question bank: practice by exam, domain or task, bookmark questions and see your accuracy per domain.
  • Mock exams: a timed exam drawn from the bank, with the real exam's length and domain mix, and a review afterwards.
  • Study plans: a reading, key terms and a short quiz for every day.

Your progress (answers, bookmarks, mock exam history) stays in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server; see the privacy page.

How the questions are written

The questions are written with Claude, an AI model by Anthropic, and the site is maintained by its owner. They are not exam dumps: none is copied or reconstructed from a real exam. The process:

  1. Each exam starts from its public exam guide: the domains, their weights and the tasks listed under each.
  2. Questions are written for one task at a time, as scenarios that ask for a decision, a comparison or a fix, and each one carries an explanation of the right answer and of every wrong option.
  3. A separate reviewer checks every batch against the vendor's own documentation (for example docs.aws.amazon.com, learn.microsoft.com, docs.cloud.google.com, docs.databricks.com, developer.hashicorp.com) and removes questions that repeat another, rely on preview features, or cannot be confirmed.
  4. Automatic checks run before anything is published: format, near-duplicates, stem length, and whether the correct answer is too often the longest option (a give-away in many question banks).
  5. When a service or an exam changes, questions are corrected in place and the change is logged; each exam page lists its recent changes.

Mistakes still happen. If you find a wrong answer, please open a GitHub issue with the question id.

Independence

cert-bank is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by AWS, Palantir, Microsoft, Google, Databricks or HashiCorp. Exam names and codes belong to their owners. Always check the vendor's official exam page for the current format, price and policies before you book.
